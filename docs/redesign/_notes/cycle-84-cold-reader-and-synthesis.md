| 6 | cluster-organization | cycle 83 (D2) | None |

**Domain-ness criterion test:** what makes a "substantive domain" load-
bearing for the matrix's NAVIGATIONAL purpose (pair selection)?

A substantive domain is navigationally meaningful when it has
SELECTIVE PAIR-APPLICABILITY — a pair that applies in this domain but
not in others. Without selective pair-applicability, the domain doesn't
inform pair selection differently than "general" does.

Walking the 6 domains against this criterion:
- **general** (1): default catch-all. Pair 8 (universal) and pair 1
  (binary-refinement-only) apply. No pair selective TO general.
- **hypothesis-eval** (2): pair 3 applies SELECTIVELY here. Genuine
  substantive domain.
- **trust-posture** (3): pair 4 applies SELECTIVELY here. Genuine
  substantive domain.
- **count-claim** (4): F1-F3 had pair 1 + pair 8 (both general).
  No pair SELECTIVE to count-claim. NOT navigationally meaningful as
  domain.
- **utility-claim** (5): F4-F5 had pair 1 + pair 8 (both general).
  No pair SELECTIVE to utility-claim. NOT navigationally meaningful.
- **cluster-organization** (6): D2 had pair 1, pair 5, pair 8.
  Pair 5 isn't selective TO cluster-organization (applies to
  multi-element distinctions generally). NOT navigationally meaningful.

**Resolution:** 2 of the 6 listed are genuine substantive domains
(hypothesis-eval, trust-posture); 4 are content categories within
general (general itself, count-claim, utility-claim, cluster-
organization). H-Q(c)'s "open-ended" framing CONFLATES these.

**Finding J-Q(b):** H-Q(c)'s open-endedness is misleadingly broad.
Axis 3 should split into:
- **Axis 3a (substantive domains, navigationally meaningful):**
  hypothesis-eval, trust-posture (closed enumeration; grows ONLY when
  new selective pair-applicability evidence surfaces).
- **Axis 3b (content categories within general, navigationally
  undifferentiated):** general, count-claim, utility-claim, cluster-
  organization, ... (open-ended; grows with each new finding but
  doesn't drive pair selection).

The matrix is NAVIGATIONAL only along Axis 3a. Axis 3b is descriptive
(catalogs content areas where findings have surfaced) but not
prescriptive for pair selection.

**v2 design-input from J-Q(b):** the 3-axis matrix is best understood
as 3.5-axis: Axis 1 (structure: multi-element vs binary), Axis 2
(generality: universal vs structure-contingent vs domain-specific),
Axis 3a (substantive domain, closed: hypothesis-eval, trust-posture),
Axis 3b (content category, open-ended). Pair selection navigates
Axis 1+2+3a; Axis 3b is descriptive metadata. v2 cycle prescription
should distinguish these.

**Honest qualification:** H-Q(c)'s "open-ended" framing isn't WRONG
— Axis 3b IS open-ended. But the framing implies navigational
significance ("matrix structure that drives pair selection") that
actually only applies to Axis 3a (closed). The conflation matters
because v2 cycle prescription based on H-Q(c) "open-ended Axis 3"
would over-state the navigational power of new content categories.
