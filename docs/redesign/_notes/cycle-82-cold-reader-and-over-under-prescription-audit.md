### G1 finding: refinement cascade discipline

Over-prescription pattern is consistently refined. The implicit
"refinement cascade" discipline is itself a v2 design-input candidate:
v2 cycle prescription should normalize the expectation that claims
are made at a rough first-pass and refined iteratively, with later
cycles catching prior cycles' over-prescriptions.

This is sibling-of-sibling pattern to cycles 78-80 stress-tests
("binary becomes more structured") — both are about claims becoming
more granular as evidence accumulates. The two sibling patterns share
a meta-pattern: **claims tend to be over-stated in the LOW-DIMENSION
direction first**, then refined to higher-dimensional structure as
evidence accumulates.

**v2 design-input from G1:** v2 cycle prescription should normalize
the refinement cascade as expected discipline. Specifically: (a) make
claims at rough first-pass without claiming exhaustiveness; (b) expect
subsequent cycles to refine; (c) treat refinement chains as evidence
of healthy discipline rather than evidence of original error.

### G2 finding: under-prescription is SYSTEMIC BLIND SPOT

Under-prescription pattern is predominantly un-addressed. Cycles
consistently produce concept/structure claims without operational
guidance.

Examples of un-addressed under-prescription:
- E1 (quaternary distinction): no operational guidance on "what
  evidence shifts factor-not-documented to factor-absent-architecturally"
- F-Q(b) (multi-lens application is norm): no operational guidance on
  "how to select lens combinations for a query"
- Cluster I re-characterized factor: no operational guidance on
  "which boundary type drives which cluster I sub-shape"
- Cluster A↔G threshold: no operational guidance on "what would
  count as A↔G intersection at multi-system convergence"

Pattern: the redesign process produces "WHAT" claims (concept,
structure, classification) consistently; produces "HOW" guidance
(operational application) inconsistently.

**v2 design-input from G2:** v2 cycle prescription should explicitly
require operational-guidance pairing with concept/structure claims.
Without this requirement, the redesign process produces "what" claims
without "how" guidance — and the "how" gap accumulates as a systemic
blind spot.

This is potentially LOAD-BEARING for v2 cycle prescription. Without
explicit requirement, future redesign cycles will likely continue the
pattern (as cycle 82 itself demonstrates by surfacing 7 un-addressed
under-prescriptions in cycles 78-81 content).

### G3 finding: meta-pattern across G1 and G2

The two patterns (refinement cascade for over-prescription;
un-addressed for under-prescription) are ASYMMETRIC. The redesign
process implicitly has discipline for catching over-prescriptions
(stress-tests, cold-readers) but lacks discipline for catching
under-prescriptions.

**Why?** Plausibly: stress-tests and cold-readers TARGET specific
load-bearing CLAIMS (over-prescription is in a specific claim).
Under-prescriptions are ABSENCES of operational guidance — they're
noticed only when someone tries to APPLY the concept and finds no
guidance. The redesign process does very little APPLYING (Phase 1 is
research; Phase 2 will be candidate generation; Phase 3 is prototype).
Until application work begins, under-prescriptions are invisible.

**v2 design-input from G3:** the asymmetry suggests v2 cycle
prescription needs an explicit "operational-guidance gap-check"
discipline (cold-reader sub-question? distinct cycle composition
shape? checklist item?). Without explicit discipline, under-
prescriptions accumulate silently. The discipline could be: "for
each concept/structure claim made in cycle N, ask: what operational
guidance is paired with this claim? If none, flag as under-
prescription for future cycles to address."

**Honest qualification on G3:** the asymmetry might also be
appropriate-stage-specific. Phase 1 is research; operational
guidance might be more appropriately developed in Phase 2 (candidate
generation) when concrete v2 prompt structures are being designed.
If so, G3's "systemic blind spot" framing is over-stated for
Phase 1 specifically and v2 design-input is more nuanced: "Phase 2
candidate generation should explicitly include operational-guidance
discipline; Phase 1 under-prescriptions accumulate as Phase 2 input."
