## Meta-observation: the operated-vs-unoperated dimension as v2 framework calibration

Cycle 128 D2 absorption surfaces a dimension the cycle 89 P1-P6 framework did not explicitly include: **does the candidate's substrate exist as operational evidence, or only as design analysis?** All three candidates were authored cycles 90-92 before any was operated. The framework's P1-P6 criteria score structural compliance with the failure-mode coverage and preservation requirements. Operational evidence does not appear because at cycle 90 no candidate had been operated.

By cycle 128, V2-era operational substrate has accumulated under A-shaped and C-shaped substrate (the V1 system was single-orchestrator; the V2 redesign-mode prompt is single-orchestrator; audit's substrate is single-orchestrator-with-cross-repo). B-shaped substrate has not been operated at all. The framework's structural-compliance lens treats this asymmetrically: B receives credit for structural-fit-on-unoperated-substrate equivalent to A/C's structural-fit-on-operated-substrate.

The empirical-vs-theoretical distinction is the calibration that surfaces this asymmetry. It could be added to the framework as a parallel scoring lens — every P1-P6 score becomes (structural-compliance, operated-evidence-or-not). At cycle 128:
- A: structural 5/5 PASS, operated-evidence YES on a long single-orchestrator-substrate trajectory
- B: structural 5/5 PASS, operated-evidence NO on multi-agent substrate
- C: structural 5/5 PASS, operated-evidence YES on single-orchestrator-substrate (C is single-orchestrator with reconcile-mode addition)

This is not part of cycle 128's edits — adding a parallel scoring lens to all P1-P6 criteria would exceed the D2 absorption scope. But the structure is there for future cycles or for Phase 3 framework refinement.

**Implication for v2 cutover deliverable:** the cutover deliverable's framework documentation should include the operated-evidence dimension as a first-class scoring axis, not just the structural-compliance dimension. Phase 3 prototype measurement will provide operated-evidence for whichever candidate Eva selects; the framework should record both dimensions explicitly rather than treating structural-compliance as the only evaluation surface.
