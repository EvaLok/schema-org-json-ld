C2 (L1.2) from cycle 152 v2-cycle-runner adversarial critique (29 findings, cycle 155 absorbed): "Step granularity is inconsistent and obscures phase boundaries — the linear step list mixes workflow phases (`reconciler-pre-poll`) and transition mechanics (`super-step-advance-*`)". Cycle 155 verdict was AGREE-WITH-CARVEOUT; the carveout was to add a phase field rather than restructure the linear step list (which works well for testing). Cycle 156's C5 added `executed: bool` to StepTrace; cycle 159 adds the paired `phase: Phase` field.

Implementation:
- New `Phase` enum (Boundary | Reconciler | Planner | Executor | Curator | Transition), kebab-case serialized
- New `phase_for(kind: StepKind) -> Phase` derivation function
- New `phase: Phase` field on `StepTrace`
- Construction site updated to derive phase from `step.kind` at trace-build time
- Phase derives from StepKind alone, independent of run mode (dry-run and live carry the same phase value per step)

Phase distribution across the canonical 10-step super-step sequence:
  step 1  super-step-init       -> Boundary
  step 2  reconciler-pre-poll   -> Reconciler
  step 3  reconciler-session    -> Reconciler
  step 4  super-step-advance-1  -> Transition
  step 5  planner-session       -> Planner
  step 6  super-step-advance-2  -> Transition
  step 7  executor-session      -> Executor
  step 8  super-step-advance-3  -> Transition
  step 9  curator-session       -> Curator
  step 10 super-step-settle     -> Boundary

= 2 Boundary + 2 Reconciler + 1 Planner + 1 Executor + 1 Curator + 3 Transition

The pre-poll is grouped under Reconciler (preparatory work for the reconciler-session, not a separate phase). Super-step-advance-N steps are grouped under Transition (control plumbing between role phases). Cycle boundaries (init/settle) are Boundary.
